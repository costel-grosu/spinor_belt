A small geometric intuition

Spinors need two turns.

A vector returns after a 360° rotation. A spinor changes sign instead, and returns to its original state only after 720°. These scenes make that hidden phase visible as flags, motion on SU(2), vector-field wires, and material wires joining concentric shells.
